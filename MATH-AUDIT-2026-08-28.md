## 1. Per-family soundness verdicts

**Extraction** (`steering/extractor.py`, `vector_math.py`, `reading_position.py`) — Sound. CAA difference-of-means, the grand-mean recipe (concept mean minus row-weighted corpus mean, concept rows included — the emotion-paper convention, fixture-pinned), mean-centering as projection removal along the neutral carrier, and alpha = scale·‖v‖/residual_norm are implemented as documented. Deliberate, stamped conventions: float32 for Swift parity, per-pair L2 normalization + mean-diff norm-matching in paired-PCA, train-majority sign rule, whole-corpus norm tally accumulated in float64.

**RepE** (`steering/repe_reader.py`) — Sound against Zou et al. PCA is genuinely mean-centered before the Gram power-iteration; PC1 is a correct unit eigenvector; the difference construction matches the reference for both contrast modes (seeded reproducible orientation replacing the reference's unseeded shuffle); sign is disambiguated with held-out pair agreement taking priority over train majority, every fallback stamped; the probe fits on train activations only; held-out accuracy is out-of-sample and provably sign-invariant; layer argmax is stamped as a recommendation, not silently selected.

**Injection/ablation** (`steering/injector.py`, `ablator.py`, `hooks.py`, `plan.py`, `residual_norm_convention.py`) — Sound. Injection is h + scale·v at the block output; scale = α × residual_norm(layer)/‖v‖ via `vector_math.norm_unit_scale`, denominator measured at block outputs of a pinned neutral corpus at the extraction's own reading position — applied consistently across sweep, condition, variant, and trainable-injector paths. Ablation is a correct float32 subspace projection h − λ·BᵀBh over a float64 modified-Gram-Schmidt orthonormal basis, one ablator per layer at the head of the chain (order-independence pinned by tests); λ deliberately never norm-denominated. Hook placement matches extraction bit-for-bit. Injection fires at the true prompt end and every decode step, with a correct chunked-prefill gate (chunk loop stops one token short, so generate's first pass is the single prompt-end firing); ablation covers every position. Composition is h₀ − λPh₀ + Δ_latent + Σαᵢvᵢ, shared-layer additions kept sequential for hashed bit-identity. All 100 unit tests in this family pass.

**SAE / J-Lens / Gemma Scope** (`steering/sae_latent.py`, `experiment/gemma_scope.py`, `optvec_jspace.py`) — Sound and unusually well-pinned by tests. JumpReLU intervention is exact: pre = h·W_enc[:,f] + b_enc with the b_dec-applied-to-input correction folded in algebraically correctly; strict-inequality upstream mask·relu gate; only the induced delta (z′−z)·W_dec[f] is decoded, so reconstruction error never enters the stream; float32 with cast-back; multi-feature edits from one read, summed; plan ordering (latent before injectors) makes composition exactly additive — each asserted by test. Both decoder-import paths apply their declared rescale conventions with distinct stamps never mixed. The J-space decomposition is an exact identity (emergent := delta − α·v, verified at l=L plus a measured linearity residual); its statistics (cosine matrices, SVD participation ratios) are basis-independent, so no orthogonality-of-basis issue arises. Genuine concerns are confined to the report-import path's seams (F9–F10).

**OptVec** (`experiment/optvec_train.py`, `optvec_gradient.py`, `optvec_campaign.py`, `steering/trainable_injector.py`) — Sound. Loss = per-role λ-weighted mean of hinged target margin (option-restricted log-softmax, provably equal to the raw logit gap) + correctly-directed KL(p0‖p_v) anchor + option-restricted capability cross-entropy. Gradient accumulation with per-role 1/count weights reproduces the step-level mean exactly; gradients flow only into the injector's parameter u (model frozen, fresh Adam per run); sphere reparameterization v = α·u/‖u‖ is on the autograd tape, so degenerate huge-norm vectors are impossible; checkpoint selection uses the val split only; the test split is firewalled behind a config-level refusal. **The critical train/serve convention is internally consistent and pinned end-to-end by tests** (deployed-vs-training-path equivalence, sphere tangency, truncated backward, finite-difference gradient checks, dose-ladder reproduction). Both confirmed defects are confined to the S3 orthogonality arm (F11).

**Cross-engine parity** (`Sources/SteeringKit/` vs Python; `steering/vector_parity.py`) — The core math is line-for-line mirrored: mean-difference/grand-mean/paired-difference-PCA, alternating-orientation and train-majority sign rules, deterministic four-start Gram power-iteration (identical degenerate-start floor 1e-6·trace), norm-unit alpha, the last-position/every-decode-pass injection gate incl. chunked-prefill suppression, double-precision MGS ablation with identical dependence tolerance, neutral-mean centering, whole-corpus norm tally, reading-position vocabulary. The `PairedDifferencePCACrossEngineTests` fixtures (Python-generated bytes, 1e-5) plus byte-identical parity goldens (1e-6) are tight enough to catch a wrong formula. Findings are edge/convention divergences, not formula errors (F13–F17).

### F1 · MINOR · Two averaging rules share the `wholeCorpusMean-v1` stamp
`Server/steerlab_server/steering/extractor.py:265-273` (independently found by the extraction and injection auditors; one defect)

The convention module (`residual_norm_convention.py:14-16`, canonical `ResidualNormTally`) defines the alpha denominator as the **per-position** mean over every measured corpus position. The denominator actually written into vector sidecars by `extract()` / `extract_grand_mean()` / `norm_backfill` comes from `activations_multi`: each capture stores the mean norm over its reading window (`recorder.py:107`), then texts are averaged with equal weight — **mean-of-per-text-means**. Both rules are stamped `wholeCorpusMean-v1` (`extractor.py:62`, `:593`; `norm_backfill.py:156`). The rules coincide for `LAST_TOKEN` (window length 1) and diverge at pooled positions (`meanFromToken`, `meanContentFromToken`) with variable-length texts — and the grand-mean default reading position on Swift is `.meanFromToken(50)`.

- Verified: Swift mirrors the same mean-of-means on the extraction path (`ConceptExtractor.swift:373-384`, `ActivationRecorder.swift:983`), so this is intra-stamp labeling inconsistency, **not** cross-engine drift. Swift's `extractGrandMean` even stamps both rules from one function (`ConceptExtractor.swift:878-889`: Tally for normSource `neutral-token-bank`, per-stimulus average otherwise). The tally today feeds only `neutral_activation_bank` → `neutral-pc-basis.json`, never an alpha denominator, so no currently-produced alpha is inconsistent.
- Consequence: at pooled reading positions, "alpha = 1 norm units" denominated via extract/backfill vs. via a future bank path are different numbers under the same stamp — the exact dose-comparability failure the stamp's own "bump the version when the averaging RULE changes" contract exists to prevent. No test discriminates the two rules (the fixture's bank stub uses equal-length texts).
- Fix shape: pick one rule per stamp string (either make the activations path position-weighted, or mint a second stamp), pin with a variable-window fixture, mirror in Swift.

### F2 · MINOR · PCA count mode has no rank cap; over-rank components are float noise
`Server/steerlab_server/steering/vector_math.py:555-558`

`_deflate`'s min_variance branch caps components at rows−1; the count branch sets cap = count with no rank bound. After full deflation the residual is float32 round-off, whose own-Gram trace is tiny-but-positive, so the relative degenerate-start floor (1e-6·trace, line 726) still passes and rounding noise is normalized into unit vectors. Verified by execution in the repo venv: 4 rows (rank 3), count=6 → 6 unit components, explained variances `[0.406, 0.310, 0.284, 7.9e-15, 4.2e-15, 2.1e-15]`. `extract()` (`extractor.py:441`) and `extract_grand_mean` (`:672`) pass user-declared `neutral_pc_count` straight through over as few as 4 neutral texts (`:425`), then `projecting_out` (`:461`) deletes the concept vector's projection onto those arbitrary directions — silent, no refusal, no variance readout on that path. The bank path (`extractor.py:131`, `min(count, cap)`) and the SVD variance path are safe.

- Fix shape: guard `min(count, rows-1)` or a typed refusal in the count branch; pin with a rank-deficient fixture.

### F3 · MINOR · Held-out and split-half diagnostics use deterministic order-based splits
`Server/steerlab_server/experiment/concept_stats.py:33-35, 51-52`

`held_out_accuracy` takes the last ~20% of each class **in file order** as the test set; `split_half_cosine` splits even/odd rows. Stimulus files authored in topic blocks make the held-out set a distinct topic cluster (pessimistic) or split near-duplicates across train/test by parity (optimistic), with no visible variance because the split is deterministic. Verified extra: the Swift twin uses a **different** deterministic scheme (`ConceptStats.swift:21`: `index % 5 == 4`), so the two engines report different heldOut numbers on identical data; nothing in `tests/test_concept_stats.py` pins the split scheme. Scope: screening diagnostics only — the true firewall (`scenario_accuracy` over validation.jsonl) is unaffected, and no coded promote-gate threshold reads heldOut (display only: `routes.py:2658`, `ConceptsPanelView.swift:3125`).

- Fix shape: seeded stratified split (or at least the same stride rule on both engines), documented; pin the scheme in tests.

### F4 · MINOR · Template-pair RepE inference score zero is not a concept-presence threshold
`Server/steerlab_server/steering/repe_reader.py:1134-1138`; `vector_math.py:440-448`

For `unsupervisedTemplatePair` readers, `score_texts` renders new text under T+ only, but the probe's center is the **midpoint of the T+ and T− train projection means** (both renderings of the same stimuli). A fresh in-distribution stimulus rendered under T+ therefore carries a systematic positive score offset ≈ |pos_mean − neg_mean|/(2·scale) — always positive, bounded by 1 via the scale floor — and `classifies_positive` labels neutral T+-rendered texts positive systematically. The `score_texts` docstring's calibration claim (lines 1121-1122) is affirmatively wrong (center and scale were fitted on both renderings' projections).

- Verified mitigations that keep this MINOR: no in-repo consumer thresholds inference scores at zero — the `repeReaderScore` analysis path (`experiment/tasks.py:11455-11462`) records a continuous endpoint compared **across conditions**, where the constant offset cancels; `_pair_accuracy` at fit time evaluates both renderings, where the midpoint is valid; supervised-content readers are unaffected.
- Fix shape: correct the docstring; document that template-pair scores support relative comparisons only (score > 0 ≠ presence); optionally calibrate a T+-only center for presence use.

### F5 · MINOR · Power iteration may exit unconverged on near-degenerate spectra, silently
`Server/steerlab_server/steering/vector_math.py:660-747` (cap at line 710)

PC1 via ≤200 power iterations, float32, max-abs-delta tolerance 1e-7, no residual check, no eigengap diagnostic, no convergence flag — an unconverged candidate is accepted silently. Reproduced empirically: an engineered cloud with sample eigenvalue ratio 0.9945 returned a "PC1" with |cos| = 0.148 against the true second eigenvector, deterministically, no warning. Verifier's calibration: at a 5% eigengap the iteration **is** converged ((0.95)^200 ≈ 3.5e-5); the problem regime is a sample eigengap under ~1%. The only degeneracy stamp downstream (`pc1ExplainedVarianceBasis: "degenerateDifferenceCloud"`, `repe_reader.py:572-577`) fires only on zero-variance clouds. Result is deterministic and cross-engine mirrored, so parity holds — the artifact just cannot tell a study PC1 was ill-defined.

- Fix shape: compute and stamp the eigengap / a convergence flag; warn (not refuse) on near-tied spectra.

## 4. Refuted claims — do not re-raise

**R1 · "Pinned converter hardcodes normalize_activations='none'; latent encode assumes raw-activation training."** The premise (Gemma Scope SAEs trained on activations rescaled to unit mean squared norm) is false. sae_lens's own canonical loader for these repos (`get_gemma_3_config_from_hf`, inspected in `Server/.venv.nosync`) hardcodes `"normalize_activations": "none"` identically — Gemma Scope releases publish no normalization field — and maps {w_enc, w_dec, b_enc, b_dec, threshold} verbatim with no input-scaling transform, exactly as the pinned converter does (`gemma_scope.py:640-641, 658-675`; the converter is actually *more* faithful: it reads `apply_b_dec_to_input` from the published config where sae_lens hardcodes False). The gemma-2 `"embedding"`-folder sqrt(d_in) case can't be silently mishandled — `_gemma_scope_hook_name` (`:580-598`) accepts only resid_post/attn_out/mlp_out and raises otherwise. RESIDUAL_NORM_MATCH_CONVENTION (`:421-434`) is a post-hoc storage-scale convention for imported decoder rows, not a claim about DeepMind's training inputs. So `SAELatentIntervention`'s raw-residual encode against the published thresholds (`sae_latent.py:232-233`) matches the trained convention exactly, and the JumpReLU gate fires at the correct scale.

**R2 · "cosine_matrix divides by zero on a zero row."** `cosine_matrix` (`optvec_geometry.py:146-152`) indeed has no zero-norm guard, but every caller enforces nonzero rows first: `observation_layers` refuses layers below the injection layer ("the delta there is exactly zero by construction", `optvec_jspace.py:368-374`); `alphaMultiple <= 0` is refused (`:232-235`); `family_report.py:381-388` builds rows only where `artifact.vectors.norm(layer) > 0.0`; `geometry()`/`fracture()` rows come via `artifact.row()`, which returns None for a zero row and triggers a typed refusal (`optvec_geometry.py:222-227, 570-575`, pinned by `test_optvec_geometry.py:190`). The `np.where` guard in `_stacked_statistics` hardens an already-guarded path, it does not evidence reachable zero rows. No reachable trigger.

## 5. Convention notes — METHODS-worthy, no code change required

Defensible, mostly stamped choices that a reader of study artifacts (or a METHODS section) should know. File:line anchors verified.

1. **Ablation is projection-to-zero, not Arditi-style state-centering** (`ablator.py:156`): h′ = h − λ·(h·v̂)v̂ at every position; the residual-mean-carrier hazard is handled by *opt-in direction centering* (`centering='neutralMean'`, warn-only |cos| > 0.35 preflight when undeclared). Subspace-removal semantics (h′·v̂ = 0) tested; an uncentered λ=1 run warns and continues (post-submit drift policy) with rows recorded as valid.
2. **bf16-quantized injection vs fp32 ablation** (`injector.py:85`): the injected perturbation is materialized once in fp32 and added in stream dtype (~0.x% dose deviation on bf16); ablation upcasts to fp32 for its reductions. Deliberate asymmetry, bit-identity of the add pinned in `test_injector_equivalence.py`. Don't expect exact dose equality across dtypes.
3. **Ad-hoc API alpha is raw scale; study alpha is norm units** (`routes.py:319`): the two surfaces denominate the same symbol differently, factor residual_norm/‖v‖. Matches the open playground ruling ("unit-blind α"); the one place the norm-units convention is not applied.
4. **RepE explained variance is over normalized differences** (`repe_reader.py:870-871`, normalization at `:853`): pc1ExplainedVarianceOfDifferences measures scatter-alignment of the unit-normalized, centered cloud — a near-perfect, highly consistent direction can legitimately stamp a *low* EV. Not comparable to raw-difference PCA EV from other toolkits.
5. **Deterministic ± alternation can couple to period-2 row-order structure** (`repe_reader.py:860`): O(1/√n) for unordered data; a dataset alternating by topic at period 2 could have a real contrast direction partially centered out of PC1. Shuffle rows or don't order at period 2.
6. **`sign_held_out_accuracy` is a selection statistic, not validation** (`repe_reader.py:887`): it's the accuracy of the sign *chosen on those same pairs* — ≥ 0.5 by construction, stamps 1.0 from two coin-flip pairs. `held_out_accuracy` itself is sign-invariant (pinned by test).
7. **`lastContentToken` doesn't verify close−1 is content** (`reading_position.py:868`): returns turn_close_index − 1 unconditionally. All currently vendored families (Gemma 3, ChatML, Llama 3) place content directly before the close marker; a future template with a structural token there would silently read structure. Latent.
8. **Neutral corpus under 4 texts is silently dropped** (`extractor.py:425`): falls back to the extraction-stimuli denominator with no neutral-mean carrier — honestly stamped (`residualNormSource='extraction-stimuli'`) but detectable only by sidecar inspection; alpha then not comparable across concepts and ablation loses its centering carrier.
9. **Token-bank determinism leans on `random.sample` stability** (`extractor.py:298`): CPython guarantees stability only for `Random.random()`; `sample()`'s algorithm may change across versions, which would drift the neutral-PC basis while every stamp says the recipe is identical. (Norm denominator immune — it counts skipped positions.)
10. **SAE doses are not in feature-activation units** (`gemma_scope.py:429`): both import conventions set the stored norm from measured residual/vector norms; latent-β dose calibration is explicit future work, stamped `doseCalibrated: false`. Equal alpha ≠ equal "feature dose" across features.
11. **`topNegative` imports steer *against* the labeled concept** (`gemma_scope.py:375`): rescale keeps the published sign (correct — flipping would misrepresent the dictionary), but the artifact's concept string inherits the analyzed concept's name unsigned; the cosine's sign lives only in the report file. A positive-alpha condition on a topNegative import measures the opposite pole of its label.
12. **OptVec anchor-KL reference comes from a different numerical instrument** (`optvec_train.py:605`): p0 cached from the stepped-KV baseline prepass, p_v from the padded batched training pass — same math, different float paths, so KL has a float-noise positive floor at v=0. Deliberate (training baseline = reportable baseline). Documented analogue: `probePassMarginDelta` is recorded, not asserted zero.
13. **"Shift rate" names two estimands** (`optvec_train.py:914`): val selection's `shift_rate` counts all target items (incl. already-target); test eval's headline `shiftRate` counts flippable items only (all-items version is `targetSelectedRate`). A val→test drop is partly definitional. The selection composite `shift_rate − max(0, meanKL − budget)` also implies an unstated 1-nat-per-1.0-rate exchange.
14. **Explained-variance neutral PCs: SVD on Python, Gram power-iteration on Swift** (`extractor.py:128` vs Swift bank): the variance-target knob is not engine-deterministic on near-degenerate spectra; bank-projected vectors agree to the 0.98-cosine parity bar, not fixture precision. Share the basis file when it matters.
15. **Parity gate is scale-blind** (`vector_parity.py:94`): pass = min-cosine ≥ threshold; normRatio computed, reported, never gated (the committed 2×-norm fixture passes). Largely mitigated because norm-unit alpha folds out ‖v‖ and ablation is scale-invariant — but a dropped ‖meanDiff‖ rescale would sail through a gate named "parity." Either gate a norm-ratio band or document scale as out of scope.
16. **Swift float32 sequential mean vs numpy pairwise** (`SteeringVectorMath.swift:349`): sequential error ~N·ε vs pairwise ~log₂(N)·ε caps cross-engine re-derived grand-mean agreement at ~1e-4..1e-3 relative in unlucky cases. 17. **Grand-mean fallback label + default reading position diverge** (`ConceptExtractor.swift:888` vs `extractor.py:637`): 'multi-concept-corpus' vs 'extraction-stimuli' labels are reconciled by `recipe_identity.py:353` canonicalization (verified).
