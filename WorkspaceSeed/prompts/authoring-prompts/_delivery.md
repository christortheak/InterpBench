## How to deliver

In this order, in one reply:

1. **The complete contents of every file named above.** Whole files, not
   excerpts and not a sample with "…and 40 more in the same style". A partial
   delivery cannot be audited, and an unaudited file is not installable.
2. **The audit report**: every number in the battery above, COMPUTED over the
   rows you just wrote, each one next to its threshold and marked pass or fail.
3. **Any rewrite you did because a number failed**, with the before and after
   counts.
4. **Your three strongest rows and your two weakest**, and why. A delivery with
   no weakest rows has not been read by its author.

Do not deliver a file whose audit fails. Rewrite first.

## What happens to this next

Nothing you produce is installed on your say-so. The emitter of a generation
prompt is never its acceptor: a separate reviewer, who did not write these
rows, re-runs this same audit battery against the delivered files and either
installs them or sends them back. That reviewer's numbers are the ones that
count, so an audit you rounded in your own favour will be found — and a
delivery that reports an honest failure is worth more than one that reports a
clean pass it did not compute.

Once installed, the file is hashed and pinned into a study manifest before any
behaviour is measured against it. After that moment it cannot be edited: a
changed byte is a pin violation, and the repair is a new study, not a fixed
file. Write it as if it were going to be frozen, because it is.
